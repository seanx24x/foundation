## Boundaries

Search covers the existing local bounded journals, with literal text and explicit filters. It does not add automatic memory, semantic retrieval, freeform documents, team sharing or cross-project search. Relationships describe shared references and do not imply causal or backend visibility. Decisions and issues keep their separate freshness and sharing rules. The native provider was a deterministic loopback fixture; remote model quality was not tested.

Compilation, observed interaction, screenshots and visual assessment remain independent. Laptop and physical keyboard-to-display acceptance stay paused at the user's request, and broad M0/M1 and platform release gates remain open.
